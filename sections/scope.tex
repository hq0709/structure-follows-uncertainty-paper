\section{Assumptions and Scope}
\label{sec:scope}
We supply the cycle size $k$ in the cyclic tasks. The counting tasks use event
bits, Gaussian features, or MNIST digits. In the valve simulator, observations
are cue tokens and the visualization uses scripted poses. The feedback study
trains learned estimators with state labels and gives the shared planner the
simulator's actuator and sensor models. The programmed counter and categorical
Bayes filter give exact references for clean and uncertain increments,
respectively.
