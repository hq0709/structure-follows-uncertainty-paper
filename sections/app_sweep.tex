\section{Full Increment-Uncertainty Sweep}
\label{app:sweep}

Section~\ref{sec:sweep} gives the shared protocol and shows how exact cyclic
transitions support learning from sparse signals. The full sweep shows how
modeling misreads keeps the cyclic filter close to matched Bayes with state
supervision.

In the missing regime, the pointer reports the true position with probability $0.8$ and
draws a uniform token otherwise. The GRU and LSTM use widths 16 and 13, with
1{,}844 and 1{,}812 parameters. The learned-angle and measured-angle rotations
use 1{,}088 and 1{,}248 parameters, respectively. The cyclic filters use
196--197 parameters. Matched Bayes uses the simulator probabilities directly.
The measured-angle rotation uses a learned angle on erased turns and a learned
input term on steps with an erasure or a pointer reading.

\newcommand{\sweepcell}[3]{\shortstack{#1 (#2)\\#3}}
\newcommand{\sweepdetails}{Cells give five-seed means for terminal NLL
(nats, with sample SD in parentheses) and terminal accuracy, with 1,024
episodes per seed and cell.}

\subsection{Increments regime}
Tables~\ref{tab:sweep-inc-dense}, \ref{tab:sweep-inc-terminal}, and
\ref{tab:sweep-inc-reward} show that the misread-aware filter tracks matched
Bayes at $L=40$ with state labels, and Table~\ref{tab:sweep-inc-long} shows
that exact cyclic transitions support exact tracking at $L=160$ from
terminal labels or reward on clean inputs.
